Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2026

Rosc is a tracker for rotating savings groups (ROSCAs/chamas). Rosc records who has contributed, whose turn it is, and group history. Rosc never holds, moves, or has access to your actual money. All payments happen directly between group members outside the app; Rosc only keeps a shared record of what group members report.

What we collect

  • Account information: the email address you sign up with, and the display name you choose.
  • Group activity: the groups you join, your role and turn order, contribution records you or other members mark as paid or confirmed, and any support tickets you send us.
  • Trust Score: a reputation score calculated from your on-time/late/missed contribution history within the app.
  • Device push token: a unique identifier used only to deliver notifications to your device (for example, a reminder that a turn is due, or a reply to a support ticket). This token is removed when you sign out.

What we don't collect

  • We do not collect or store payment card numbers, bank account details, or any financial account credentials.
  • We do not access your device's contacts, location, camera, or photo library.
  • We do not sell or share your data with advertisers or third-party marketers.

How your data is used

Your data is used only to operate the app: showing your groups, contribution history, and Trust Score to you and to the other members of your own groups, and sending you notifications you'd reasonably expect (turn reminders, ticket replies, group activity, Trust Score changes).

Where your data is stored

Data is stored with Supabase (a hosted database provider) and notifications are delivered via Firebase Cloud Messaging. Both providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy commitments.

Your choices

You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting support@packisher.com, or by deleting an incomplete signup directly in the app before finishing setup.
