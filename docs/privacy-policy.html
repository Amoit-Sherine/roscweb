ROSC Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026
What is ROSC? ROSC is an app that helps a small group of people run a rotating savings arrangement (also known as a chama, merry-go-round, susu, stokvel, or ROSCA) by tracking who has paid, whose turn it is, and how the group is doing overall. ROSC never holds or moves money itself.

ROSC is currently in closed testing (TestFlight / Google Play Closed Testing) with a small group of invited testers. This policy describes how ROSC handles personal information during this testing period and going forward. It is written in plain language for testers and reviewers.

Who this policy covers

ROSC ("we", "us", "the app") is an independent app built and maintained by an individual developer, not a registered company. ROSC helps a small group of people ("a circle") run a rotating savings arrangement, sometimes called a chama, merry-go-round, or ROSCA, by tracking who has contributed, whose turn it is to receive the pooled amount, and each member's reliability over time. This policy applies to anyone who uses the ROSC mobile app.

This policy is written to meet the principles of Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, since our testers are based in both countries.

Information we collect

We collect only what the app needs to function:

  • Account information: the email address (or phone number) you sign in with, verified by a one-time code. We do not store passwords.
  • Profile information: your display name, age, region (used to suggest a default currency), preferred currency, and an optional avatar image.
  • Payment handle: the Interac e-transfer email or M-Pesa phone number you choose to show other members when it's your turn to receive a payout. ROSC never processes, holds, or moves money itself. Members pay each other directly, outside the app.
  • Group and financial-activity data: which groups you belong to, your position in the payout order, contribution amounts, payment and confirmation timestamps, and your Trust Score (a reliability score based on paying on time and completing cycles).
  • Device and notification data: a push-notification token tied to your device, so we can notify you about activity in your groups. If you use Face ID/biometric app-lock, that check happens entirely on your device. We never receive your biometric data.
  • Support requests: anything you send us through in-app support, so we can help you.

We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and we do not collect location data.

How we use this information

  • To operate the core features of the app: creating and running savings groups, tracking contributions and payouts, and calculating Trust Scores.
  • To send you notifications about activity relevant to you (for example, a payment was recorded, it's your turn, a vote is open). You can mute categories of notifications in Profile settings at any time.
  • To respond to support requests.
  • To fix bugs and improve the app.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers.

Who can see your information

Members of a group you join can see the information relevant to running that group: your display name, avatar, payout position, contribution/confirmation status, and, only when it's your turn to receive a payout, your payment handle. Your Trust Score becomes visible to other members only after your first full cycle completes. The ROSC team can access data as needed to operate, debug, and support the app.

Where your information is stored

ROSC is built on Supabase, a third-party infrastructure provider. Our database and backend functions currently run in a United States data centre (Oregon). This means your personal information is transferred outside both Canada and Kenya as part of normal operation of the app. Supabase encrypts data in transit and at rest, and we restrict access to your data using row-level security so that, in general, you and relevant group members can only see what's necessary for the group to function.

How long we keep your information

We keep your account and group history for as long as your account is active, so that group ledgers stay accurate and disputes can be resolved. If you ask us to delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal information within a reasonable time, except where we need to keep limited records (for example, a completed group's ledger) to preserve the integrity of other members' financial history in a shared group.

Your rights: Canada (PIPEDA)

If you are in Canada, you have the right to:

  • Know why we collect, use, or disclose your personal information;
  • Access the personal information we hold about you;
  • Request correction of inaccurate information;
  • Withdraw consent, subject to legal or contractual restrictions and reasonable notice;
  • Challenge our compliance with this policy by contacting us (see below).

Your rights: Kenya (Data Protection Act, 2019)

If you are in Kenya, you have the right to:

  • Be informed of the use to which your personal data is to be put;
  • Access your personal data in our custody;
  • Object to the processing of all or part of your personal data;
  • Correction of false or misleading data;
  • Deletion or destruction of your personal data that is inaccurate, out of date, incomplete, or unlawfully obtained, or that we are no longer authorized to retain;
  • Data portability, where technically feasible;
  • Lodge a complaint with Kenya's Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) if you believe your rights have been violated.

Security

We use Supabase's built-in encryption (in transit and at rest) and database-level access controls to protect your information. No system is completely secure, and we ask you not to enter real financial account passwords or highly sensitive information anywhere in the app.

Children's privacy

ROSC is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

This policy may change as the app changes. We will update the "Last updated" date above when it does, and significant changes will be communicated to users directly.
