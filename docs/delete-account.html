Delete Your ROSC Account

Last updated: September 7, 2026

How to request deletion

Account deletion in ROSC is currently handled as a reviewed request, not an instant self-service action. To request it:

  1. Open the ROSC app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete my account.
  4. Confirm the request.

This submits a request to our team through the app's support system. We review and action deletion requests within a few business days.

Don't have the app installed, or can't sign in? Email us directly (see Contact below) from the address associated with your account and we'll process the request the same way.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in credentials
  • Your profile information (display name, age, region, preferred currency, avatar, payment handle)
  • Your device push-notification token

What may be retained

If you were a member of a shared savings group with other users, we keep the minimum contribution and payout records tied to that group (amounts, dates, and confirmation status) so that the group's ledger stays accurate for the other members, and so we can accurately respond to a Trust Score or payment dispute. Personally identifying details are removed or anonymized from these records wherever the integrity of a shared group's history allows.
